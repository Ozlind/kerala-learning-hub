This map identifies official starting points. It is not a complete chapter inventory. A chapter is not considered verified until its exact textbook, medium, edition/year and chapter title are recorded.

## Known revision context to account for

- SCERT's revised textbook rollout has covered Classes 1, 3, 5, 7 and 9 first, then Classes 2, 4, 6, 8 and 10. Do not assume all classes use the same edition year.
- SCERT's current portal also lists 2026 Class X model questions and 2026–27 Plus One scheme-of-work material. These should be checked before building exam preparation resources.
- Plus One includes a broad list of language and elective textbooks. Plus One and Plus Two must be modelled as subject/stream-specific catalogues rather than a single generic subject list.
- Samagra's official KITE description says it covers Classes 1–12 and provides digital resources such as videos, audio, interactive content, digital textbooks and question banks. Link to individual resources and record their exact metadata.
